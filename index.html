How to submit in Discord Developer Portal

To verify your bot or fulfill App Directory requirements, submit both links in your application settings:

  1. Open the Discord Developer Portal.
  2. Select your bot application and navigate to the General Information tab.
  3. Scroll to Terms of Service URL and paste the copied Terms link.
  4. Scroll to Privacy Policy URL and paste the copied Privacy Policy link.
  5. Click Save Changes at the bottom of the portal.
